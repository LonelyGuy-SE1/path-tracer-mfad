Architectural Pipeline (Initial Baseline to Final Result) Steps 1 — 6
Step 1: Camera & Raster Grid Issue #19
Primary ray generation and raster-to-camera coordinate mapping. Validates bottom-left (0,0) scanline inversion, field-of-view aspect projection, and sky dome interpolation.

Step 2: Analytical Primitives & Materials Issue #21, #25
Closed-form ray-sphere and ray-plane intersections. Validates Duff et al. orthonormal basis cosine sampling (diffuse), Snell's law refraction (dielectric glass), and specular reflection (metal chrome & gold).
Geometry: 4 Spheres + Plane Samples: 128 spp
Step 3: Global Illumination Benchmark Issue #26
Canonical Cornell Box verification fixture. Validates multi-bounce diffuse interreflection (pink & green color bleeding on white walls), energy conservation, and Russian roulette termination.
Geometry: 6 Quads + 2 Spheres Samples: 256 spp (32 bounces)
Step 4: Direct Illumination Engine Issue #24
Explicit O(N) shadow ray evaluation. Validates acne-free origin offsetting, Lambertian cosine factors, distance attenuation, and crisp directional occlusion.
Lighting: Analytic Point Light Samples: 16 spp
Step 5: Loaded Scene (Direct Lighting) Issue #20, #24
Dynamically parsed JSON scene (cube mesh from cube.obj, glass ellipsoid egg, glass sphere) with singular corner light. Crisp directional shadows and deterministic Whitted glass transmission.
Lighting: Corner Key Point Light Noise: 0% (Deterministic)
Step 6: Final Destination (Path Traced) Issue #27, #60
Full Monte Carlo Path Tracer with Next Event Estimation (area light sampling) and Russian roulette. Soft penumbra contact shadows, glass caustics, and subtle diffuse interreflection.
Lighting: Corner Physical Area Light Samples: 512 spp (16 bounces)
